Process

From discovery to deployment

A defined delivery process keeps design decisions, technical requirements and business outcomes aligned from the first conversation through launch.

1. Discovery

Clarify the audience, services, goals, required features, content and practical launch constraints.

2. Structure and design

Map the information architecture, establish the visual direction and prototype key interactions.

3. Development

Build the responsive frontend, backend features, integrations and conversion paths.

4. Testing

Check devices, browsers, forms, links, accessibility, metadata and performance.

5. Launch

Deploy securely, connect analytics and search tools, submit the sitemap and verify production behaviour.

6. Improvement

Use real visitor and enquiry data to prioritise future content and conversion improvements.
